What is the volume of a sphere in n dimensions? I wanted to find out, so I took the disk method from single-variable calculus and extended it to higher dimensions.

The paper walks through the full derivation. It uses multivariable integrals, trigonometric substitution, and the Gamma function to reach a closed-form result. I typeset it in LaTeX as the final project for MATH 253 in the Skyline College Honors Transfer Program.
